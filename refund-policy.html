Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for bookings made through AS Tour and Travels. Please read it before confirming any booking with us.

1. Third-Party Cancellation Rules Apply

Train, flight, bus and hotel bookings are governed by the cancellation and refund rules of the respective operator — Indian Railways/IRCTC, the airline, the bus operator, or the hotel. These rules (including cancellation charges, cut-off times, and non-refundable fares) are set by the operator, not by us, and will apply to your booking regardless of the reason for cancellation.

2. Our Service Charges

Any service or convenience charge collected by AS Tour and Travels for assisting with your booking is separate from the ticket/room fare, and is non-refundable once the booking has been processed, since this covers the time and effort of arranging your booking.

3. How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel a booking, message us on WhatsApp at +91 82985 00120 or call us as early as possible, quoting your booking/PNR details. Earlier requests have a better chance of a higher refund, since most operators charge more the closer you cancel to the travel date.

4. Refund Timelines

Once a cancellation is processed with the operator, refunds (where applicable) are released by the operator/payment gateway and typically take 5–10 business days to reflect in your original payment method. We will share the refund reference with you once cancellation is confirmed, but we do not control how long the bank or gateway takes to complete the transfer.

5. Tour Packages

For tour packages, cancellation charges depend on how close to the departure date you cancel, and on the hotel/transport/vendor terms already booked on your behalf for that package. Exact charges will be shared with you at the time of booking confirmation.

6. Passport / Visa Assistance

Government/embassy fees paid as part of a passport or visa application are non-refundable through us once submitted, as these are paid directly to the relevant authority. Our assistance/service charge for this support is also non-refundable once work has begun on your application.

7. No-Show & Missed Travel

If you miss your train, flight, bus or check-in without cancelling in advance, standard "no-show" rules of the operator apply, which are typically less favourable than an advance cancellation. We are not able to override these rules.

8. Important Note

We do not guarantee a full or partial refund in every case — the final amount refunded depends entirely on the fare type, timing of cancellation, and the specific operator's policy at the time.

9. Contact Us

For any cancellation or refund query, reach us at jharaja538@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 82985 00120.
