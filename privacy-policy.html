Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AS Tour and Travels ("we", "us", "our") operates as a travel booking assistance service based in Darbhanga, Bihar. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from you, how we use it, and how we protect it when you contact us through this website, WhatsApp, phone or email.

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire about or book a service with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and WhatsApp number
  • Travel details such as origin, destination, travel dates and class/room preference
  • Passenger details required for ticket booking (name, age, gender, ID proof where applicable)
  • Passport/visa related documents, only where you request assistance with that service
  • Payment-related details necessary to process your booking (we do not permanently store full card or bank details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To search for, quote, and book train, flight, bus, hotel, cab or tour package services on your behalf
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or booking via WhatsApp, phone call or email
  • To share your details with third parties (such as IRCTC, airlines, hotels, bus operators, or visa facilitation centres) strictly as required to complete your booking
  • To maintain basic booking records for support and any future reference

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. Your details are shared only with the relevant travel or government service provider needed to complete the specific booking or application you asked for, and with no one else.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including limiting access to your details to the people directly handling your booking. However, no method of transmission over WhatsApp, phone, email or the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

You can ask us at any time, over WhatsApp, phone or email, what information we hold about you, and request that we correct or delete it, subject to any records we are required to keep for completed bookings.

6. Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party services such as Google Maps or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party platforms.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
